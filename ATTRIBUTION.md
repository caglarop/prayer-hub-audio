# Bundled recordings — sources and licences

## Quran recitation (`quran/abdulbasit/1.mp3` … `6236.mp3`)

- **Reciter:** Abdul Basit Abdus-Samad, Murattal style.
- **Source:** Islamic Network CDN (alquran.cloud), edition `ar.abdulbasitmurattal`, 64 kbps —
  `https://cdn.islamic.network/quran/audio/64/ar.abdulbasitmurattal/{ayah}.mp3`, numbered by
  global ayah (1–6236). Verified on 30 September 2026: file 1 is byte-identical (MD5
  `a592c3b486291b927fe98f32e94899be`) and the ID3 tags read "Served by Islamic Network CDN".
  Islamic Network's recordings originate from everyayah.com.
- **Licence (alquran.cloud Terms & Conditions, section IV, retrieved 30 September 2026):**
  > "Recitations are licensed to us by the reciters or their estates for free, non-commercial
  > redistribution at the bitrates we publish." — "You may stream, embed and download them for
  > personal and educational use." — "You may bundle them into a commercial product, but please
  > note that copyrights lie with the reciters and they may ask you to remove the content."
- **everyayah.com practice:** credit the reciter and everyayah.com, non-commercial use, do not
  alter the recordings (cutting/streaming is fine, no remixing or pitch/speed changes).
- **How Prayer Hub complies:** the files are redistributed unchanged at the published 64 kbps
  bitrate, the app is free and ad-free, and the reciter and sources are credited in the app
  (Quran page footer, `quranAudioCredit`) and here. **If the app ever becomes commercial (paid,
  ads, in-app purchases), obtain permission from the reciter's estate first or replace the
  recitation.** Copyright remains with the reciter.
